STAGE 1 of 3: Replace the current drag mechanism with framer-motion's drag
system so post-its follow the cursor smoothly during drag.

IMPLEMENTATION:
1. In the post-it component, replace the wrapping element with motion.div
   from framer-motion.
2. Set drag={true}, dragMomentum={false}, dragElastic={0}.
3. Use onDragEnd to commit the final position to state/storage. The handler
   receives (event, info) — use info.point or compute from the element's
   transform; do NOT rely on event.clientX alone.
4. Preserve the existing rotation: rotation must remain applied via the same
   style/transform mechanism it currently uses, NOT replaced by framer-motion's
   rotate prop. The post-it should drag with its rotation intact.
5. Tap-to-complete must still work. Use framer-motion's onTap (not onClick)
   so a small drag does not register as a tap. Threshold: framer-motion's
   default is fine.
6. While dragging, raise the post-it visually:
     - whileDrag={{ scale: 1.05, zIndex: 50, boxShadow: "0 12px 24px rgba(0,0,0,0.18)" }}
   The non-dragging shadow should remain whatever it currently is.

DO NOT in this stage:
- Do NOT implement frame constraints yet (that's Stage 2). It's fine if
  post-its can still leave the frame after this stage.
- Do NOT implement auto-sort (Stage 3).
- Do NOT change how positions are persisted — only how they're updated
  during interaction.
- Do NOT animate the rotation. Rotation stays static per post-it.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA (verify in browser preview before reporting done):
- Clicking and holding a post-it, then moving the mouse: the post-it tracks
  the cursor in real time with no perceptible lag.
- Releasing the mouse: post-it stays where it was dropped, no snap-back, no
  jump.
- Single click without drag: post-it gets marked complete (existing behavior).
- Rotation is preserved during and after drag.
- Works on both the main board and the archive page.
- Refreshing the page: post-its are at their last-dropped positions.
