Going with Tauri. Here's why and the constraints:
Decision rationale:

Personal-use app, single user (me), occasional self-pushed updates.
Want a real installer file, not a PWA.
Care about install size and RAM footprint since I'll have this open all day.
Tauri's native webview wins on resource cost; ~8MB installer vs Electron's ~80MB matters for a tool I'll iterate on.
Tauri's GitHub-Releases-based auto-updater fits the "occasional updates" workflow without infrastructure.
